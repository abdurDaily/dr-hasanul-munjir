About the Dentist

Gentle hands, honest advice, lasting smiles.

Dr. Hasanul Munjir · BDS (CU) · PGT (OMS), Chattogram Medical College

For more than a decade I've helped families in Chattogram feel calm in the dental chair. I explain every option, show you your X-rays, and only recommend what your teeth truly need.
